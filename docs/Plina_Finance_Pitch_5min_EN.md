# Plina Finance
**5-Minute Pitch**

*Fabrício Santos & Thais Reis — contato@plina.finance — plina.finance*

---

## 1. What We Do *(30 sec)*

Plina Finance connects global capital to Brazil's consortium credit rights market through institutional tokenization on Stellar. Our product, PLINA-RF, is a tokenized pool of awarded consortium quotas, structured as a regulated fund and distributed to institutional investors worldwide via USDC and EURC.

---

## 2. The Problem *(30 sec)*

Every month in Brazil, thousands of people receive a consortium credit award worth R$ 50k to R$ 500k — and need the cash immediately. The secondary market is slow, opaque, and dominated by informal middlemen. It takes weeks to get paid.

On the other side, international institutional investors have never been able to access Brazilian credit rights. No structure, no compliance controls, no entry point.

---

## 3. The Solution *(45 sec)*

We buy awarded quotas from sellers and pay them via Pix within 48 hours. We pool those credit rights into a formally regulated fund and issue PLINA-RF tokens on Stellar — with native clawback, KYC controls, and compliance flags built in.

A European family office deposits USDC, receives PLINA-RF, and gets exposure to Brazilian credit yields — without opening a Brazilian bank account or navigating local KYC. No one else offers this.

---

## 4. Market *(30 sec)*

Brazil's consortium market is R$ 1.2 trillion — the largest in the world, with no equivalent anywhere else. There are R$ 180 billion in awarded quotas in circulation right now. Our target pool for the first three years is R$ 2 billion. The barrier to entry is regulatory and structural; this market cannot be replicated outside Brazil.

---

## 5. Why Now *(30 sec)*

In March 2025, Brazil's CVM suspended a competitor's consortium tokens for operating without proper structure. The suspension was later overturned on procedural grounds — but the message was clear. The regulator wants formal structure. CVM Resolution 175 now provides the exact legal framework we're building on. The window for the first mover with proper institutional setup is open today.

---

## 6. Business Model *(30 sec)*

We make money three ways: an origination spread on every quota we buy, a 2% annual management fee on AUM, and a 20% performance fee above benchmark. Revenue scales directly with pool size. The more quotas we originate, the better the yield for investors — and the more we earn.
