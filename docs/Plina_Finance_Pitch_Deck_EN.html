Why Now

The regulatory window just opened.

In March 2025, Brazil's CVM suspended a competitor's consortium tokens for operating without proper structure. The suspension was later overturned on procedural grounds, but the message was clear: the regulator wants formal structure.

The Framework

CVM Resolution 175 gives us the exact legal framework we build on.

First mover with institutional setup. Open today.
